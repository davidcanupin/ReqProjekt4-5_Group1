FA1 – Glossary “EV Charging Network”

Requirements Engineering Project · Group 1 · Version 1.0 (draft)

This glossary defines the unique terms used throughout the project (User Story Map, Epics & User Stories, Domain Model, Feature Files, Step Code and Java Business Objects). The column Synonyms lists the alternative terms found in the customer needs document; these synonyms are not used in project artifacts.

1. Roles

TermDefinitionSynonyms (customer needs)
Owner The private entrepreneur who operates the EV Charging Network. The Owner manages Locations, Chargers and Prices, monitors the Network Status and can view the Transaction History of every Customer Account. CPO (Charge Point Operator), Operator, Private Entrepreneur, Network Owner
Customer A registered person who uses the Chargers of the EV Charging Network to charge an electric vehicle. A Customer owns exactly one Customer Account. Client, User, Driver

2. Network & Infrastructure

TermDefinitionSynonyms (customer needs)
EV Charging Network The entirety of all Locations operated by the Owner. The software solution manages and bills the EV Charging Network. Network, Charging Network, Ladenetzwerk, Elektro-Tankstellen-Netz
Location A physical site of the EV Charging Network with a unique name and address. A Location has one or more Chargers and its own Price List that applies to all of its Chargers. Site, Charging Station, Electric Charging Station, EV-Charging-Station, Station
Charger A single charging point at a Location to which exactly one electric vehicle can be connected at a time. Every Charger has a unique identifier, one Charging Mode (AC or DC) and a Charger Status. Charging Point, Charge Point, EVSE (Electric Vehicle Supply Equipment), EV-Charge-Point, Charging Bay, Charging Unit
Charging Mode The type of electric current a Charger delivers: AC (alternating current, slower charging) or DC (direct current, fast charging). The Charging Mode determines which Prices apply. Charger Type, Type, Current Type, AC/DC
Charger Status The current operating state of a Charger. Possible values: Available – ready to start a Charging Session; Occupied – a Charging Session is in progress; Out of Order – the Charger cannot be used. State, Operating Status, Operational State
Network Status An overview list, available to Owner and Customer at any time, showing for every Location the currently valid Price List and the Charger Status of each Charger. Status List, Current Status, Station Overview

3. Pricing

TermDefinitionSynonyms (customer needs)
Price List The set of Prices of one Location, consisting of the Energy Price and the Parking Price for each Charging Mode (AC and DC). The Owner can change a Price List at any time; it applies to all Chargers of that Location. Different Locations may have different Price Lists at the same time. Station Prices, Prices, Tariff
Energy Price The price per kilowatt-hour (EUR/kWh) of Charged Energy, defined separately for AC and DC. kWh Price, kWh AC / kWh DC, Price per kWh
Parking Price The price per minute (EUR/min) of Charging Duration, defined separately for AC and DC. Time Price, Parking Price per Minute, Price per Minute
Price Lock Rule that a Charging Session is always billed with the Price List valid at the start of that Charging Session; later price changes do not affect it. “prices at the beginning of the loading process apply”

4. Customer Account & Billing

TermDefinitionSynonyms (customer needs)
Registration The process in which a person creates a Customer Account. Only registered Customers can top up and charge. Sign-up, Register
Customer Account The prepaid account of a Customer that stores the Balance and all Transactions. A Customer Account must have sufficient Balance before a Charging Session can start (prepaid system). Account, Client Account, Prepaid Account
Top-Up A Transaction in which a Customer adds money to the Customer Account in advance. A Top-Up increases the Balance. Deposit, Pre-load, Prepaid Load, Recharge
Balance The current amount of money available in a Customer Account: the sum of all Top-Ups minus the sum of all Charging Costs. Credit, Account Balance
Charging Session One charging process of a Customer at one Charger, from start to end. A Charging Session records Location, Charger, Charging Mode, start time, Charging Duration and Charged Energy, and results in a Charging Cost. Loading Process, Charging Process, Charging Service, Loading, Charge
Charged Energy The amount of electric energy transferred to the vehicle during a Charging Session, measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh). Loaded Energy, Energy, kWh
Charging Duration The time in minutes from start to end of a Charging Session. Charging Time, Duration of Use, Parking Time
Charging Cost The amount billed for one Charging Session: Charged Energy × Energy Price + Charging Duration × Parking Price, using the Price List of the Charger’s Location under the Price Lock. The Charging Cost is deducted from the Balance. Charging Expenses, Cumulated Prices (kWh & Parking), Charging Fee
Billing The calculation of the Charging Cost at the end of a Charging Session and its deduction from the Customer Account. Bill, Invoicing
Transaction A single entry in a Customer Account: either a Top-Up or a Charging Session with its Charging Cost. Account Activity Entry, Booking
Transaction History A date-sorted list of all Transactions of one Customer Account. Each Charging Session entry shows Location name, Charger, Charging Mode, Charging Duration, Charged Energy and Charging Cost (energy & parking). The Transaction History ends with the current Balance. Visible to the Customer and the Owner at any time. Account Activity, EV-Charging-Ledger, Ledger, Transaction-History, Prepaid-Load Items
